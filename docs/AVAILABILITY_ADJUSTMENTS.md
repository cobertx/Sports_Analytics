# Historical availability adjustments

NBA/NFL dashboard game forecasts (PPP, EPA, ELO) and player forecasts (WHP,
UO, OAM, PGE) now apply a common historical availability overlay when supported.
FanDuel, Kalshi, Polymarket, Market Summary, Quick Filtering and new bet-slip
estimates use the same forecast helpers. Existing bet-slip legs and previously
saved filtered results retain the estimates recorded when created.

## Current availability and time cutoffs

Structured public ESPN NBA/NFL `/injuries` responses supply player, club, status,
report timestamp and any expected return date. No API key is required. This is
an unofficial public endpoint, with no guaranteed schema or availability; it is
not a complete roster/transaction service. Headlines are never interpreted as
participation probabilities. Only reported Out/IR/PUP/suspended statuses enter
the adjustment. Questionable/doubtful statuses remain at baseline and are shown
as uncertain. A report's absence is not evidence that a player is healthy.
An Out report with an expected return on/before a later game is not carried into
that game. The window is 48 hours for NBA and seven days for NFL.

The source is fetched in calculation workers, with a 3-second connection and
6-second read timeout, shared by concurrent methods, and cached for five minutes.
Failures retain the baseline and are retried after a cooldown. Navigation does
not fetch injury data. Live forecasts use the current report with the original
saved snapshot's historical-statistics cutoff: availability can be newer than
the displayed odds. The UI shows the retrieval timestamp.

Source packets are saved to `data/information_variable/availability/<sport>.json`
and immutable `archive/<sport>/<UTC date>/` snapshots. For past games, only an
archive retrieved before the earlier of quote capture and kickoff, and no more
than 30 minutes old at that cutoff, is eligible. Today's status is never replayed
into old games. No archive means the original historical forecast.

## Historical participation

The overlay reads existing saved histories, without downloading player history
on navigation. Use Prepare player history to populate saved players and the
existing UO preparation workflow for team boxes. NFL also requires the saved
all-position `data/modeling/nfl/raw/snap_counts_<season>.csv` generated by NFL
game-model preparation. A missing participation ledger leaves the baseline
unchanged and explains which preparation is needed.

NBA team-game boxes must reconcile total player minutes with regulation/overtime
and total player points with team points, with at least seven participants.
Rounded minutes allow a 2.5-minute tolerance; this is a box completeness check,
not an official inactive roster. NFL uses the all-position snap ledger with
coverage checks; a player missing from an offensive production table is never
assumed absent. Exact normalized name and club, a unique local ID, observed
club tenure and at least a meaningful historical role (12 NBA minutes or 10 NFL
offensive/defensive snaps on average) are required. Ambiguous identities and
stale-club matches are skipped. Earlier unknown tenure is excluded rather than
zero-filled. Trading away and back ends the earlier stint.

“Not playing” means no observed participation in an otherwise complete game
during inferred club tenure. It does not establish whether the reason was an
injury, rest, suspension or a coaching decision. Missing/incomplete records are
unknown. This distinction is retained in the evidence table.

## Team outcomes

For each reported absent player, compare team points scored and allowed in
playing/nonplaying games. Descriptive win rates, averages and game counts are
shown. The numerical adjustment uses a joint ridge regression of scoring on
absence indicators, with opponent, home venue, season and calendar-trend
controls. Recency and the existing 0.2 prior-season weight apply. The injury
coefficient penalty is 10 and nuisance-control penalty 20, expressed relative
to effective sample weights; these are research defaults, not tuned claims.

At least twelve complete observations, three games in each participation state
with effective N at least two, and three observations of the proposed joint
absence pattern are required. Multiple players are estimated together; an
unseen joint absence is not assembled by summing independent penalties.

The change is relative to recent observed availability (last five NBA or three
NFL eligible games), rather than assuming the original forecast describes a
fully healthy team. If that recent window already contains the absence
throughout, no further deduction is applied. This is an approximation to the
different baseline models' weighting, not an exact decomposition of each fit.
The scoring adjustments update both score means; parameter-estimation
uncertainty is added to margin and total variances, including the covariance
between scoring and points allowed. Market probabilities are recomputed from
the adjusted distribution.

## Player statistics

For Player B, estimate minutes (NBA) or passing attempts, rushing carries or
receiving targets (NFL) in games with and without Player A. Use only B's
current-club appearances and the same joint comparison/support rules. Convert
the estimated workload change into a production change using B's original
forecast mean divided by recent observed workload. This assumes production per
opportunity remains at the baseline rate; it does not estimate every possible
efficiency, opponent or defensive-personnel interaction.

The production change and its estimation uncertainty are convolved with the
original predictive distribution on integer outcomes. Fractional deterministic
shifts split mass between neighboring integers. Push mass and Over/Under
settlement remain complementary. Empirical mass is retained exactly; analytic
tails are retained in boundary bins at least ten standard deviations out.
Nonnegative statistics are censored at zero; signed NFL yardage remains signed.
Combo props receive the common player workload change on their original joint
statistic distribution. Reported unavailable players are excluded, not assigned
an automatic Under win: nonparticipation and void settlement are separate.

These are marginal adjustments. They do not rebuild the joint UO/OAM/PGE team
allocation, promise conserved adjusted team minutes/targets, or introduce
validated cross-player parlay correlations.

## Evidence and validation

The game/player market page includes an **Injury & roster adjustment** section
with original-to-adjusted forecasts, playing/nonplaying averages, counts, win
rates where relevant, and recent absence share. Skipped/uncertain inputs remain
visible. Quick Filtering, exchange and summary status messages show one concise
game injury list (team, player and status), without repeated model-adjustment explanations.
Cache identities include the original forecast, input revisions, source packet
and implementation version; original model objects and saved quotes are not
mutated.

The mathematical baseline engines, research experiments and existing backtest
metrics remain unchanged. Their accuracy must not be presented as validation
of this overlay. Prospective evaluation needs archived pregame availability
and matched original/adjusted forecasts on the same games. Opponent/trend
controls and shrinkage reduce confounding but do not prove a causal injury
effect. Weather, uncertain participation mixtures, minutes restrictions,
automatic transaction integration and replacement-specific efficiency remain
future work.

Offline checks: `python scripts/check_availability.py` covers both sports,
all seven methods, source/cache concurrency, historical cutoffs, incomplete
participation, established absences, joint-support requirements, forecast
isolation and integer probability conservation.
